About FAZZ

Technology should open doors, not add barriers.

Future Advancements in Zero Barrier Zone — a student-led nonprofit out of Frisco, Texas.

Mission

Build accessible tools that make learning and everyday life easier.

Vision

A community where good technology is available to everyone in it.

Values

Collaboration, accessibility, and shipping things people actually use.

Our approach

Not a portfolio. Not a startup.

FAZZ is a place where students learn by building things that get used — then hand them to the community for free.

  • Find a real need We start with a problem someone actually has.
  • Build it together Students design, code, and test in small teams.
  • Put it in their hands Free to use, and supported at in-person events.

Technology can illuminate possibilities, reveal solutions, and empower the people around us.
